Roadmap to the platinum

Five phases, in the order that wastes the least travel. The phases are built from the official trophy descriptions — which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts — and from the published roadmap estimates cited on the Sources tab.

    Save like you mean it

    There is no mission replay. The game gives you 15 manual save slots and one autosave: keep a save at the start and end of every chapter, keep one from before the final mission until the meditation counter reads 18, and rotate the rest in free roam.

    Cheats are off the table

    Cheats disable trophies — and saving. Keep them off the save you are running for the platinum, entirely. A cheat session is a separate save or it is a ruined one.

    Two habits pay for everything

    Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study every new animal through the binoculars, from the first hour. Those two habits quietly finish four trophies and the hardest quotas of the 100% checklist.

    Missables — one real risk, several false alarms

    This list has exactly one trophy that can be missed, and it is missable because of a single moment in Chapter 1. Everything else that looks time-limited is documented below so you do not waste a restart on it.

    Looks missable — is not

    • Strand givers who vanish. Kenton goes quiet after Chapter 1 and others pause mid-chain. Every trophy strand giver returns and stays after the story; a silent marker is a gate, not a loss.
    • The bowling operation. The Kingpin Bowling robbery behind Let's Go Bowling can be attempted again if it goes wrong — the operation comes back.
    • Timed and expiring quests. Optional missions with an hourglass can fail or expire, but none of them is tied to a trophy, and the game offers far more optional missions than the 100% checklist's 100.
    • Finishing the story. Free roam continues after the credits and everything the 100% checklist needs stays reachable. The ending locks away nothing but the Chapter 1 meditation.
    • The Irredeemable profile. Reaching it is permanent and makes police more aggressive — it makes cleanup harder, never impossible, and blocks no trophy.

    Sources and method

      How to read the citations

      Every trophy's guide names its sources at the foot of its entry in the tracker. Names, descriptions, grades and tracked counts are PlayStation's, exactly as published; the words of each guide are this app's own. Where the published guides disagree — the size of the card-set quota for 100%, whether the Chapter 1 meditation counts — the entry says so rather than picking a side silently. Two trophies (Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There) had no confirmed method in any cited guide when this was written; their entries say that plainly and give the best current theory, marked as one.
